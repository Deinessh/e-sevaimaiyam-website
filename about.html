Bridging Government Portals & Citizens in Minjur

Common Services Centres (CSC) act as physical access points for delivering essential digital public utilities, social welfare schemes, healthcare, financial, education, and agriculture services directly to citizens.

Our mission in Minjur is to establish a simple, transparent, and stress-free service delivery mechanism. We reduce the need for citizens to make multiple trips to government offices by providing complete documentation, verification support, online filing, and official printouts under one roof.

Secure & Authenticated Submissions
